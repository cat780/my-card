A LITTLE HELP

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for your visit to Catalina's digital trading card.

How do I exit the personality quiz?

Click or tap anywhere outside the quiz and the trading card. The quiz panel will close. To open it again, select Personality Quiz on the home page.

How do I flip the trading card?

On a device with a mouse, hover over the card. With a keyboard, tab to the card and focus it to see the back.

Can I take the personality quiz again?

Yes. After you see your result, choose Take the quiz again to start over with a fresh set of answers.

What does “Activate ability” do?

It toggles the card's special ability message. Select it again to return to the original message.

Where can I send another question?

Visit the support page for contact details and a form to draft an email.
